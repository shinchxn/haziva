## 1. Executive Summary

This forensic audit documents the architecture, implementation, empirical test results, evidence controls, and baseline database isolation of the HAZIVA Incident Simulation engine.

The Incident Simulation module enables disaster commanders to test hypothetical severe rainfall scenarios (e.g. cloudbursts, monsoon spikes) through the **exact trained production Machine Learning inference pipeline** (`Model A`) and feed the resulting simulated risk into the **actual relocation intelligence engine**.

#### Explicit Distinction: ML Features vs. System Architecture Features

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Features INSIDE the Random Forest ML Model (11 features):**
> - Copernicus 30m DEM Terrain Slope (`slope_degrees`)
> - GSI 1:50k NLSM Susceptibility & Coverage (`gsi_susceptibility`, `gsi_coverage`)
> - ESA WorldCover 30m Land Cover Fractions (8 fraction features: `tree_fraction`, `shrub_fraction`, `grass_fraction`, `crop_fraction`, `builtup_fraction`, `bare_fraction`, `water_fraction`, `wetland_fraction`)
>
> **Features OUTSIDE the Random Forest ML Model (System Architecture Layer):**
> 1. **Dynamic Rainfall & Rainfall Stress** ($S = 1 - e^{-\alpha R}$): Applied dynamically at runtime to scale spatial susceptibility $P_{\text{ml}}$ to forward forecast windows ($R_{\text{dynamic}} = \min(1.0, \max(P_{\text{ml}}, P_{\text{ml}} \cdot (1 + 1.2 \cdot S)))$).
> 2. **Census 2011 Population & Exposure**: Used in the **Village Exposure & Prioritization Engine** (`calculate_priority`) to compute human vulnerability and priority ranks ($P_1, P_2, \dots$), NOT inside the Random Forest terrain classifier.
> 3. **OpenStreetMap Facilities & Road Proximity**: Used in the **Relocation Intelligence Engine** to evaluate candidate sites, nearest OSM road distances, and carrying capacity, NOT inside the Random Forest classifier.
